Areas of interest

01

Stochastic topology

  • Topological phase transitions
  • Limiting distributions of topological invariants
  • Universality in random topology
  • Homological percolation and connectivity
02

Applied topology

  • Topological noise modelling
  • Statistical topology: inference, filtering, hypothesis testing
  • Complex networks and signal processing
  • Natural language processing
03

Probability & stochastic processes

  • Point processes and random geometric graphs
  • Limit theorems for local functionals
  • Random fields and Morse theory
  • Poisson approximation
