How to read it

The picture shows the excursion set Au = {f ≥ u} of a smooth Gaussian random field.

Moving right on the barcode lowers u and the set grows. Each bar is one component, handle or cavity: it starts where the feature appears and ends where it merges away or fills in. Drag across the barcode to change u; the Betti numbers are the counts of bars crossing the orange line. Point at or tap a bar to see its numbers.
