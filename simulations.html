Persistence Explorer

Grow a ball of radius r around every point and watch the topology change: components merge, loops appear and fill in, and every feature leaves a bar recording its lifetime. Rips and alpha complexes, barcodes and persistence diagrams, and a representative cycle highlighted in the cloud when you click a bar.

  • H₀ and H₁
  • Rips and alpha complexes
  • Cluster persistence

Point Process Lab

Draw three samples side by side, each from a point process of your choosing, then pick a functional and compare how its values are distributed across the three. Uniform, Gaussian, annulus, sphere, torus and mixture samples in 2D and 3D, with empirical CDFs or densities on linear, log or log-log axes.

  • H₁ and cluster persistence
  • k-NN degrees
  • Nearest-neighbour ratios
